Payments — wireframe

Low-fi, but built from the live tokens and the existing SignupWizard card (max-w-md rounded-2xl bg-paper p-6). Steps stay login → name → pay → keys. Only the name step's price row and the pay step body change; both live in nail's lib/payments module. Revised Oct 25 2026: there is no currency picker anywhere. formstr shows exactly one price itself — the fixed dollar price — everywhere, always, before any payment method is chosen; the buyer only ever chooses a payment method ("Pay with Bitcoin" or "Pay in your local currency"), never a currency. Sats only appears once "Pay with Bitcoin" is actually selected and a BOLT11 invoice is generated. Once "local currency" is picked instead, the same fixed dollar price is charged and Stripe Adaptive Pricing (Card) or the store's own purchase sheet (Apple/Google) handles localisation — formstr never computes or shows a converted number itself. Red pins 1 mark what is new versus today's UI.

1 · Name step — price row

Same tier card. The dollar price is the only price formstr itself shows here — no currency select, no sats, nothing fetched from Stripe yet (it's plain config, not a quote). Sats only appears later, on the pay step, once "Pay with Bitcoin" is actually chosen. Store builds show the store's own localized price instead (set once in the console, not computed by us).

2 · Pay step — web & direct APK

The title ("One payment, and it's yours") and "Claiming alice@…" subtitle are unchanged. The body gains the two-choice method picker, both options showing the same dollar figure until one is actually picked; Lightning is today's InvoiceQR, moved into the package untouched. Nothing is fetched from Stripe until "Pay in your local currency" is selected, and the Bitcoin option only turns into a sats amount once it's selected and a BOLT11 is generated.

3 · Pay step — store builds

No picker, no Lightning, no Stripe. One button that opens the native payment sheet.

4 · Shared states (all rails)

After any rail reports done, the wizard waits for the backend to say "paid" — never for the client's own belief. Card polls GET /api/orders/:sessionId/status (the Checkout Session id); store purchases need no polling because POST /api/store/verify answers synchronously (provisioned / already_provisioned / needs_attention); Lightning keeps its WebSocket.

5 · Workspace seats panel (mail client → Settings → Workspace)

Today's SeatsPanel: a quantity input and a total. Open builds keep that and pay through the same payment sheet as §2. Store builds replace the free quantity with a fixed pack picker (consumable purchases of 1, 10 or 50 seats) at the store's price. This panel uses the mail client's own styling, not the landing card.

Notes

Pins — 1 dollar price only here, no currency select, no sats until Bitcoin is actually picked · 2 CTA no longer names a method · 3 store-localized price · 4 fixed dollar price, no quote/expiry · 5 Stripe-hosted iframe — we style only the surrounding card, Adaptive Pricing does the rest · 6 APK hosted-checkout hand-off · 7 App Review requirement.

Dark mode / in-app modal — the buy flow inside the mail client renders under .landing (BuyAddressModal), which pins the light palette, so this wireframe's single light theme is correct for both surfaces.
Accessibility — the picker is a role="radiogroup" with arrow-key navigation, matching the tier cards' role="radio" pattern; the Android back button closes the sheet via the existing back stack (no new overlay).
